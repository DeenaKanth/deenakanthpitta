# Application Tracker

Maintained by the `job-marketing` agent. Statuses: `Saved` → `Applied` →
`Recruiter screen` → `Interviewing` → `Offer` / `Rejected` / `Withdrawn`.
Follow-up rule of thumb: nudge 7 days after applying or after an interview
with no response.
